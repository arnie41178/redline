The main risk for anyone who makes IP

Broad IP assignment

The sentence it came from

“Contractor hereby assigns all intellectual property, whether created before or during the engagement, to the Client.”

What it does to you

“Before or during” sweeps in work you made before this client existed, including your tools, templates and portfolio. You can't take it back later, which is why it's Critical.

Counter-offer you can paste

“Contractor assigns only the deliverables created specifically for this project. Pre-existing materials, tools, and templates remain Contractor's property, licensed to Client for use of the deliverables.”

What Redline won't tell you.

Redline stays inside these limits on purpose, because a tool that guesses past them can get you hurt.

Whether a clause holds up in court.
