# 06: Drafted counter-offers for Flags and Gaps

**What to build:** For every Flag and every not-found Gap, the reader gets
drafted **counter-offer** language — wording they can paste into their own reply
to the counterparty. One capability spanning both finding types: a Flag's
counter-offer replaces the dangerous clause; a Gap's counter-offer proposes the
missing protection.

Export is **copy-paste only**. Redline does not send, deliver, or share anything
with the other party, and does not share a document between users — that is
excluded on purpose (`CLAUDE.md`). A future agent must not add a
"send to counterparty" or shared-document channel: the reader copies the drafted
text into whatever reply channel they already use.
